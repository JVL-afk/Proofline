Privacy

Last updated 6 September 2026.

This page describes how the Proofline website (proofline.business) handles information. It is written to state only what this website actually does.

What this website does not do

  • There are no user accounts and nothing to sign up for.
  • There is no contact form. Contact is by email.
  • Proofline does not intentionally add marketing cookies to this website.
  • Proofline does not add advertising pixels or advertising trackers.
  • Proofline does not carry out behavioural profiling of visitors.
  • Proofline does not sell personal information.

Technical information

Proofline does not intentionally add advertising or behavioural-tracking technologies to this website. Our hosting infrastructure may process ordinary technical information required to operate and secure the site — for example, server or content-delivery-network request logs. This is standard for any website and is used to keep the site available and protected, not to build profiles of individual visitors.

Contacting Proofline

If you email andrew@proofline.business, that message and your email address are kept so the enquiry can be answered and followed up. They are not added to a marketing list and are not shared for unrelated purposes.

If you have received email from Proofline and would prefer not to, reply with the word UNSUBSCRIBE and the address will be removed.

Questions

Privacy questions can be sent to andrew@proofline.business.
