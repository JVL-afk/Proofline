**Status:** Complete — synthetic control plane validated; all live permissions disabled

## Objective

Implement a separate deterministic control plane for future Texas Commercial HVAC shadow
validation without accessing a real business, source, person, contact, provider, or delivery path.

## Authority boundary

M6.7A owns cohort policies and manifests, stage-lineage projections, reviews/QA, metrics, costs,
safety incidents/stops, replay assessments, and synthetic M6.8 evidence packages. It reads exact
M1-M5 canonical projections and cannot mutate them. It has no M6 command or persistence model, no
M6.6 AI input, and no external-network or delivery port.

The Phase 1 ceiling is `CONTACT_PHASE_NOT_AUTHORIZED`. `SHADOW_READY`, `SEND_READY`,
`SEND_AUTHORIZED`, and `SENT` are structurally unreachable.

## Implemented scope

- Exact, independent successor permission taxonomy with all states initialized
  `NOT_AUTHORIZED`; legacy `REAL_COMPANY_RESEARCH` grants nothing.
- Frozen 24-company synthetic cohort machinery with Texas/B2B/Commercial-HVAC eligibility,
  deterministic deduplication, franchise cap, multi-location lead-flow units, a frozen seed,
  reserve order, predetermined replacements, and attempted-slot lineage.
- Read-only deterministic M1-M5 projection with exact revision/hash lineage, mandatory opportunity
  and artifact reviews, explicit UNKNOWN/PROPOSED economics, and no upstream bypass.

- Existing-state terminal outcome projections, seeded negative QA, strata coverage, measured review
  duration, explicit metric numerators/denominators/states, and a stage cost ledger with USD 0 AI.

  overrides a safety stop.
