## Objective

Prove the exact lifecycle from one CONTENT_APPROVED M5 revision through synthetic identity,
proof-scoped contact verification, fixture eligibility, readiness, human one-message authorization,
mock provider acceptance, receipts/replies, first-party assertions, and controlled re-analysis.

## Authority and exclusions

M1-M5 remain read-only and authoritative. M6 uses only reserved synthetic data and a zero-network
mock provider. It contains no real person/enrichment/verification/email/webhook provider, legal rule,
live sender, credential, message, AI, CRM, calendar, telephone/SMS, bulk send, sequence, autonomous
follow-up, or M7 behavior. A-17 remains the separate mandatory live-activation gate.
