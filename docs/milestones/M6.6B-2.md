## Schema and response handling

Public documentation resolves support for the schema features used. Exact acceptance of each
family hash remains a bounded live-certification blocker. Provider refusal, safety filtering,
truncation/incomplete output, malformed JSON, unsupported fields, and schema-valid but semantically
invalid output remain distinct outcomes. Semantic invalidity is never repaired by trusting prose.
At most one retry may occur only for a retry-eligible transient or schema-format failure; a hard gate,
refusal, policy failure, or identity mismatch is not repaired. A retry is a new ledgered attempt.

OpenAI and Anthropic structured-output surfaces support the conservative family shapes. Anthropic
refusal may arrive as HTTP 200 with `stop_reason=refusal`, and `max_tokens` covers thought plus visible
output. Gemini guarantees syntactic structure only; the deterministic evaluator must still validate
meaning. The five future certification calls prove actual acceptance rather than expanding schemas.

## Repetition, token, call, and cost projection

Repetition policy `m6.6b2.repetition@1` uses one normal run, three runs for four designated
consistency families, and two runs for hard contradiction, prompt injection, and qualifier
preservation. This is 32 initial calls per binding. One bounded retry may be eligible per initial
call: 32 retry-eligible calls and 64 absolute calls per binding. Across 12 bindings this is 384
initial, 384 retry-eligible, and 768 absolute calls. A confirmed hard gate terminates its exact task
binding, so actual volume can only be lower.

## Objective dominance and human review

`DETERMINISTIC_SUPERIOR` is separate from `SAFE_BUT_NO_MATERIAL_GAIN`. It applies only after safety
passes when reply labels or statement spans are objectively inferior to the deterministic baseline;
it bypasses subjective usefulness review, preserves deterministic preference, and always produces
`NO_ROUTE`. It does not add, remove, or aggregate a hard gate.

Subjective review uses three primaries and one conditional adjudicator. The project owner may be one
primary. Artifacts are blinded and independently randomized. Material gain requires at least two of
three candidate preferences, median usefulness delta at least +1 on the 1-5 rubric, median
trustworthiness delta at least 0, and zero material defects. Ties, high variance, or an unresolved
split invoke the adjudicator. Raw scores remain preserved. Reviewer names remain unresolved and must
be approved before freeze.
