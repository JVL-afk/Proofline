# M6.10 — Recipient & Channel Resolution Engine

Owner authorization 2026-09-06 "M6.10 RECIPIENT & CHANNEL RESOLUTION
ENGINE". Formally opens M6.10: given a qualified company/opportunity,
automatically identify the best appropriate business decision-maker(s),
discover available professional contact routes, verify them, apply
legal/privacy/policy constraints, rank eligible channels, and produce an
evidence-backed contact plan while refusing unverified or unauthorized
routes. Not an autonomous phone-calling or bulk social-outreach system.
