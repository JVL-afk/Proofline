## 2. Two legitimate launch paths (the owner chooses; this ADR does not)

### PATH A — FORMAL-CERTIFICATION-FIRST

**NO-GO** until the deferred M6.8-3 work is completed:

1. POS / morphology-aware **generative canonical reconciler** (replaces the
   enumerated-glue reconciler; must NOT be gate-fit by adding words to the glue
   set);
2. **strength-increase narrowing** (the `strength_increase` false-positive class);
3. **checkpoint / resume audit-chain seeding** (seal the resume seam so a
   `CERTIFIED_SAFE` run has one continuous hash chain);
4. one clean bounded **`CERTIFIED_SAFE`** `claude-sonnet-5` run.

Then return to M6.9 with a certified transformer and re-run this review.

*Cost:* an owner-authorized, bounded engineering task (reconciler rework) +
one more paid certification run. *Benefit:* first contact proceeds only behind a
formally certified transformer.

### PATH B — BOUNDED HUMAN-REVIEWED FIRST-CONTACT EXCEPTION

Permit **exactly one** real first contact despite M6.8-3 remaining formally
`NOT_CERTIFIED`, **only if** the PROJECT_OWNER explicitly signs an exception
acknowledging the formal blocker. This path is extraordinarily bounded:

- exactly **one recipient**;
- exactly **one company** — the complete M6.8-4 matrix points to **A-Plus** as
  the only package distinctive and coherent at both the communication and the
  demo layer; Elite is `SHADOW_SAFE_BUT_WEAK` (demo `BESPOKE`, communication
  weak); E+M is non-distinctive at both layers (`NOT_DISTINCTIVE_ENOUGH`
  communication + `GENERIC_CAPABILITY_DEMO`);
- **exact-version-bound** communication artifact (bound to `body_sha256`);
- **exact-version-bound** demo brief (`demo.builder_brief@3.2`, bound to
  `underlying_brief_sha256`);
- **human review of every rendered word** immediately before use;
- **no automated sequence**; no autonomous regeneration after approval;
- **no tracking**; no bulk sending; no automatic follow-up; no CRM automation;
- **kill switch remains available** at all times;
- **any content change invalidates the approval** and requires a fresh signature.

*Cost:* the owner personally accepts the formal blocker for one bounded test.
*Benefit:* a real first-contact signal without waiting on the reconciler rework.

**This ADR does not choose between A and B.** M6.9 exists to make that owner
decision explicit and signed.

## 3. Demo-link policy conflict — explicit owner decision required

The M6.5 delivery policy previously prohibited **links, attachments, and
tracking**. The new customer experience (M4 V3.x) depends on an **interactive
demo**. M6.9 must **not silently assume demo links are allowed.**

> ### DECISION REQUIRED: `DEMO_LINK_AUTHORITY`
>
> - **Option 1 — `NOT_AUTHORIZED`** (default): the first contact contains **no
>   demo link**. The email describes the simulation concept in prose only; a
>   demo is shown later, in a separate channel, only if a reply invites it and a
>   further owner decision authorizes it.
> - **Option 2 — `AUTHORIZE_ONE_NO_TRACKING_DEMO_URL_FOR_EXACT_APPROVED_FIRST_CONTACT`**:
>   one demo URL may be included, and **only** if it:
>   - points only to the **exact approved demo** (the `demo.builder_brief@3.2`
>     A-Plus rendering, bound by hash);
>   - contains **no tracking parameters** of any kind;
>   - contains **no personalized secret / customer data** in the URL;
>   - does **not imply target-business ownership or affiliation**;
>   - remains **clearly labelled** "Simulation · not connected to {{business}}";
>   - does **not collect** real customer / contact data;
>   - contains **no analytics** unless separately approved.

**This ADR does not alter the M6.5 delivery policy.** It only surfaces the
decision. If Option 2 is chosen, a separate change to M6.5 is required first.

## 5. Contact-eligibility prerequisites (defined here; not started)

Contact resolution **remains entirely out of scope**. No search for owner /
president / GM / operations / service manager / email / phone / LinkedIn / any
recipient occurs until a signed GO path explicitly authorizes it.

M6.9 **may define** the future contact-eligibility requirements:

- a single, role-appropriate decision-maker (owner / GM / operations lead) at the
  exact target business;
- a **publicly listed business** contact channel (no scraped personal address);
- verification that the recipient and the business match the frozen envelope
  identity (display name + hostname);
- no prior opt-out / suppression;
- the recipient bound to the exact approved artifact hash before final review.

## 6. Exact future first-contact lifecycle (planning only — not executed)

```
GO signed (Path B, or Path A completed + re-reviewed)
  -> resolve exactly one eligible decision-maker (public channel only)
  -> verify recipient identity matches the frozen envelope
  -> bind the exact recipient to the approved artifact hash
  -> final human review of every rendered word (subject + body + any link)
  -> single controlled send (one recipient, one message, email only)
  -> observe result
  -> NO automated follow-up without a separate owner approval
```

Each arrow is a manual, human-gated step. There is no automation, no sequence, no
regeneration, no tracking at any step.

## Consequences

- **Positive:** the launch decision is explicit, signed, and bound to exact
  hashes; the M6.8-3 blocker cannot be silently bypassed; the demo-link policy
  conflict is surfaced, not assumed.
- **Negative:** Path B accepts a formally uncertified transformer for one contact
  on the owner's personal signature; Path A delays first contact by an
  engineering task.
- **Risk:** an owner signature on Path B without resolving the disclosure slots
  or legal review would produce a non-compliant send — mitigated by making
  slot resolution + legal sign-off explicit prerequisites (§7.4, §7.5) and by
  "any content change invalidates the approval".
