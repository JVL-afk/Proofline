# Phase 1 A-17 Qualified Legal Review Package

**Status:** `AWAITING_QUALIFIED_LEGAL_REVIEW`
**Scope:** US-TX, Commercial HVAC, company-level public research, no contact or communication
**Repository analysis is not legal advice or approval.**

## Exact processing description for counsel

The proposed controller/operator would process an owner-approved candidate manifest and exact
unauthenticated first-party public business websites for a 24-company Texas Commercial HVAC
calibration cohort. It may retain incidental public names or professional contact strings only
inside restricted immutable source captures necessary for fidelity. It does not extract, index,
search, measure, verify or project those values into person/contact or M6 records. It performs no
communication, form/booking/chat/mailbox action, browser fallback, authenticated access, bypass,
people enrichment, AI processing or delivery.

## Questions requiring explicit written conclusions

1. Does this exact operator and Phase 1 processing fall within the Texas Data Privacy and Security
   Act's controller/processor scope? Which public-information, small-business, entity-only, processor
   or other exclusions/exemptions apply, and to which data classes?
2. Does incidental public person/contact content in restricted immutable website captures qualify as
   personal data, publicly available information, or another statutory category for this purpose?
   Does source fidelity change that conclusion?
3. What privacy notice, consumer-right request, correction, deletion, appeal, processor-contract or
   data-protection-assessment obligations, if any, attach before capture begins?
4. Is the proposed data minimization design—no person/contact extraction/index/search/projection and
   exact-source/retention gates—sufficient for applicable Texas duties? What specific change is
   required if not?
5. Do the proposed 30/90/180/365/730-day retention values, 30-day backup overhang, deletion
   tombstones or renewable legal holds create additional Texas obligations or require shorter
   periods?
6. Does Texas Business & Commerce Code Chapter 521 apply to any captured field or selected system?
   If so, what reasonable-security, destruction, incident or notification control materially changes
   the proposed architecture?
7. Does the Texas Data Broker Act or another Texas privacy/data statute apply even though the system
   does not sell, contact, enrich or create person/contact records? State the factual trigger relied
   upon.
8. For `www.tdlr.texas.gov/LicenseSearch/`, may the proposed automated or operator-assisted business-
   eligibility validation access, store and reuse the exact approved fields? Identify any statutory,
   terms, licensee-person or redistribution restriction.
9. For the Texas Comptroller account-status service, may the exact future public API/interface be
   used for targeted entity validation? Identify endpoint-specific terms, attribution, field,
   storage, matching or reuse restrictions.
10. For each first-party business host, what site-terms, robots, copyright, access, capture, excerpt
    or reuse review is required? Does public accessibility plus compliant robots handling remain
    insufficient without a source-specific conclusion?
11. Does preserving immutable HTML/text for 90 days and evidence excerpts for 180 days present a
    copyright/licensing issue? What excerpt or access restrictions are required?
12. Do the selected cloud, region, subprocessors, backup location, access model or incident process
    create notices, contracts, cross-border, breach-response or retention requirements?
13. Confirm that this research approval does not authorize later recipient discovery, commercial
    email, calling, forms, booking or other outreach, and identify any separate future review needed.
14. State the effective date, jurisdictions, assumptions, exact policy/source/environment versions,
    expiry/review date and events that invalidate the opinion.
