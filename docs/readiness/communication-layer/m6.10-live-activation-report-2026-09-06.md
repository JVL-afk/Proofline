## 2. Exact controls encoded (module: `opintel_contact_resolution.counsel_controls`)

- **Directories (§2):** `DirectorySourceKind` distinguishes
  `BUSINESS_PROFESSIONAL_DIRECTORY` (permitted) from consumer people-search/
  profile-database/ad-tech/location-tracking (all blocked,
  `directory_source_permitted`). `DIRECTORY_DURABLE_FIELDS` limits retained
  fields to name/title/employer/work_email/business_phone/source/
  retrieval_date (`minimize_directory_result`).
- **Enrichment (§3):** `enrichment_permitted_for_person` requires the role
  to already be confirmed from a first-party or approved-directory source —
  never from enrichment itself. `VendorComplianceChecklist` (8 required
  fields — lawful-sourcing warranty, per-record provenance, no sensitive
  categories, no minors, no scraped credentials, deletion-on-request,
  vendor contractual controls/indemnity, Texas registry screening) must be
  fully satisfied before any specific vendor may activate.
- **Texas Data Broker Registry (§4):** `TexasDataBrokerRegistryCheck`
  record type; `disposition` is never inferred from `registry_result` alone
  — registration is not treated as proof of lawful sourcing.
- **Ingest minimization (§5):** `minimize_directory_result` (stricter than
  the general `minimize_raw_result`).
- **Email verification (§6):** `classify_verification_method` splits
  passive-allowed (syntax/corporate-domain/DNS-MX/provider-confidence/
  catch-all) from deceptive-prohibited (pretexting/impersonation/deceptive
  contact/unauthorized access) and **fails closed on anything unrecognized**.
  `verification_is_expired` enforces the 90-day expiry.
- **Pattern-derived email (§7):** `PatternCandidateSet` structurally allows
  exactly one `chosen` candidate (raises `ValueError` otherwise — no
  permutation spraying); `is_corporate_domain` rejects personal-provider
  domains; `is_generic_role_mailbox` rejects info@/sales@/etc. substitution;
  `CatchAllDetectionResult.blocks_candidate` drops catch-all/inconclusive
  results; `DomainBounceMonitor.suspended` disables a domain once its bounce
  threshold is exceeded; `pattern_email_eligibility_disposition` applies
  all of these before the general eligibility gate even runs.
- **LinkedIn/profiles (§8):** `SourceConflict` +
  `resolve_first_party_vs_linkedin_conflict` implement
  `FIRST_PARTY_COMPANY_SOURCE_WINS`. Delivery stays `MANUAL_HUMAN_ONLY`
  structurally (`CHANNEL_DELIVERY_MODE`, unchanged).
- **Phone (§§9-11):** `phone_kind_from_provenance` returns `None` (excludes
  the candidate) when a source cannot distinguish a direct line from a
  personal mobile; `PhoneReachability.INDIRECT` is the default for any main
  line. `phone_execution_disposition` downgrades any resolved-eligible phone
  disposition to `RESOLVED_BUT_EXECUTION_NOT_AUTHORIZED` whenever
  `PHONE_OUTBOUND_EXECUTION_AUTHORITY` is not granted — which it structurally
  never is yet.
- **Cross-channel suppression (§12):** `opintel_suppression.SuppressionRegistryService.suppress_person`
  + `check_channel_blocked` (already built in Stage A/M6.10) implement
  person-wide propagation; `suppress_channel` stays narrowly scoped to one
  channel unless a person-wide/company-wide entry also exists.
- **Retention/purge (§13):** `RetentionPolicy` enforces the 30-90 day
  counsel range at construction time (`ValueError` outside it);
  `DEFAULT_RETENTION_POLICY.purge_after_days == 60`.
  `SqlAlchemyContactEndpointEvidenceRepository.purge_expired` deletes aged
  rows unconditionally; the separate, permanent `opintel_suppression`
  registry (hash-keyed, never purged) is what actually keeps enforcing a
  suppression indefinitely — purging contact evidence never lifts it.
- **Deletion/provenance requests (§14):** `build_deletion_provenance_report`
  + `SqlAlchemyContactEndpointEvidenceRepository.delete_for_person`
  implement the `HOW_DID_YOU_GET_MY_INFORMATION`/`DELETE_MY_INFORMATION`
  workflow, regardless of whether TDPSA applies to the individual.
