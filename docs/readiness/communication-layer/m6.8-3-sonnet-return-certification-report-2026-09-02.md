## 2. The bounded rule (sections 2–3)

`comm.output_validator@5`, `contract="v2"` only. `_redundant_wrapper_reconciled()`
lets a **source-less** manifest entry avoid `claim_manifest_source_mismatch`
**only** when every deterministic condition holds:

- it overlaps ≥ 1 sibling entry whose rendered span is a strictly-finer part of
  its own (`_wrapper_covering_siblings`);
- every content word of the wrapper span is either covered by a sibling or is
  non-substantive framing / attribution / transition / glue — any residual
  substantive word ⇒ hard fail;
- the wrapper-only residual (span minus covering siblings minus licensed numeric
  strings) carries **no** digit / currency / percent; is **not**
  fact / inference / recommendation-bearing (a bare "While reviewing X's public
  pages, I noticed" shell is recognised by `_is_framing_leadin`, not treated as a
  claim); trips **no** prohibited / availability / demo / response concept
  (negation-aware, matching rule 8); contains **no** economic or contact
  language;
- **every** covering sibling that renders fact-bearing wording is itself validly
  licensed — resolvable, non-empty `ref`s at ≥ `OBSERVED_PUBLIC_TEXT` — and the
  wrapper does not assert stronger evidence than its siblings, nor does any
  sibling over-assert vs its own source.

The provider's declared `claim_type` is **never** trusted (section 3): a
`DISCLOSURE` / `TRANSITION` label cannot hide an unlicensed fact — the decision
is purely on spans, sibling coverage, source licenses, and deterministic
classification. When the exemption applies, an **advisory**
`provider_manifest_type_mismatch` ("source-less wrapper span fully reconciled by
sibling entries") is recorded and the hard finding is suppressed. If any
substantive unit remains uncovered, the existing hard `claim_manifest_source_mismatch`
still fires.

## 8. Why it is NOT_CERTIFIED, and why this is where M6.8-3 stops

For the fourth `claude-sonnet-5` certification attempt, the **provider-authored
claim manifest — not the prose — is what fails.** The `@5` reconciliation rule
worked exactly as authorized: it cut the manifest-family failure from ~40% of
candidates (Attempt-6 / earlier sanity) to ~7% here, lifting the pass rate to
0.90. The residual ~6/81 are **genuine Sonnet manifest deficiencies** the bounded
rule correctly does **not** rescue:

- a substantive fact folded into a source-less entry with **no granular sibling**
  (s05, 3/9);
- the opening observation sentence given **no manifest entry** (s07 r0, s01 r5);
- one inference entry **over-asserting** its source strength (s09 r7);
- one CTA the parser cannot classify against the structured intent (s01 r5).

None reaches the reader as an unlicensed or unsafe claim — they are defects in
the manifest artifact the model is asked to author alongside the email. The
manifest-mismatch ceiling is 0.00 and the zero-tolerance set includes the
manifest-honesty codes, so a ~7% stochastic manifest-defect rate is
`NOT_CERTIFIED`.

Per section 8, the 81-call run surfaced the **residual tail of the known
manifest-discipline family**, not a new provider-behavior family — so, per
section 8's "STOP instead of iterative gate-fitting" and section 10, this is
where M6.8-3 stops. No further validator changes were made.

### Recorded for future architectural consideration (section 9 — NOT for M6.8-3)

Whether the provider should author the claim manifest at all, or whether it
should be **derived deterministically** from the validated prose + the semantic
envelope (the model writes only subject + body; a deterministic pass span-matches
each clause to the fact / inference / recommendation that licenses it). Every
Sonnet certification attempt (5, 6, 7, return) has failed on the model-authored
manifest while the prose was acceptable or better. This is not implemented now.
