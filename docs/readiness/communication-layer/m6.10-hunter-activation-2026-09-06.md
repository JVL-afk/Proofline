"data broker" definition to any business that "collects, processes, or
transfers personal data that the business entity did not collect directly
from the individual." Hunter crawls the public web for professional email
addresses it did not collect from the individuals and licenses that data to
customers — it very plausibly falls within the expanded definition and
would be required to register. Whether it *has* registered is a material,
unverified fact. This task will not invent it (§2), and will not treat an
unverified status as acceptable (a legal-risk judgment outside this task's
