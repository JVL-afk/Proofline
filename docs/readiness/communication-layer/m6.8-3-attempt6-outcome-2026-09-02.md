## 4. Why it still fails

### `structure_violation` — 63 occurrences, the dominant blocker

Almost entirely **body length**: mean body 136.4 words, 58 / 81 candidates
between 132 and 168 words against the hard 130-word ceiling that `@6` states
explicitly ("body: at most 130 words"). Plus ~5 subjects at 61–71 chars against
the 60-char ceiling. `claude-sonnet-5` overshoots a stated hard limit by a
median ~6 words. This is a soft instruction-following miss on a hard cap, not an
envelope-safety failure — but it is a hard-fail by contract and it is present in
essentially every rejected candidate.

### `unlicensed_claim` — 10 occurrences (zero-tolerance)

Representative uncovered tokens: `reviewing`, `noted`, `listed`, `listing`,
`came`, `describing`, `available`, `what's`, `sorted`, `though`, and business
possessives `hvac's` / `systems'`. These are the model's **framing / attribution
verbs** in sentences like "While reviewing <Business>'s public pages we noted a
listing for…". This is the same class the round-2 residual attribution
identified: `_licensed_framing_vocab` covers hostnames and the engine's
`usage_rule` words but not generic review/observation verbs, and rule 12 fires on
a zero-tolerance code. Arguably still validator overbreadth rather than genuine
fabrication — flagged for owner, no change made.

### `claim_manifest_source_mismatch` — 10 occurrences (zero-tolerance)

All "claim mN cites an unknown or empty source" (15b): a FACT / INFERENCE /
RECOMMENDATION manifest entry with `licensed_source_ids = []` or an invalid id.
Mixed: some are genuine manifest omissions on a substantive claim; some are
framing sentences the model typed FACT that `_classify` does not affirmatively
recognise as non-fact-bearing (so the §4 15b waiver does not apply). Manifest
honesty ceiling is 0.00, so any occurrence fails.

### Drift — 9 / 9 scenarios

Every provider-reaching scenario produced 9 distinct response fingerprints and an
unstable validator-finding signature across its 9 repeats: the safety outcome is
not deterministic run-to-run. Classified `PROVIDER_MODEL_DRIFT` (the served model
identity was constant `claude-sonnet-5` throughout — this is
SAFETY_OUTCOME_STOCHASTICITY, the naming the Attempt-5 forensic flagged for a
future policy revision; no change made here).
