```
STRUCTURAL REQUIREMENTS (hard - a candidate that violates any of these is rejected):
  - subject: at most 60 characters; no 'Re:' or 'Fwd:' prefix.
  - body: at most 130 words. Count words in the body text only; the '{{...}}'
    placeholder lines do not count and must be kept verbatim.
  - exactly ONE call-to-action sentence (one '?' permission ask). If you fold in a
    canonical discovery question, keep it inside that same one CTA sentence - do not
    add a second '?' sentence.
  - the subject and the body are ONE atomic candidate; the subject may not say
    more, or sound more certain, than the body.
  - keep every '{{...}}' placeholder exactly as given, on its own line, unresolved.

COMMUNICATION-POLICY CONSTRAINT FOR THIS RUN:
  - Do NOT restate or paraphrase any RESPONSE_COMMITMENT fact (a licensed fact
    whose category is 'response_commitment', e.g. 'we answer every call', 'we aim
    to return inquiries promptly', 'same business day') anywhere in the subject or
    body - not even attributed as 'your site says'. Treat those facts as
    present-but-not-to-be-rendered. All other licensed facts, findings, inferences,
    recommendations, UNKNOWNs, and prohibited claims are unchanged.
```

And, in the CLAIM MANIFEST SCHEMA section, the `licensed_source_ids` line now
states which entry types take `[]`:

```
  - "licensed_source_ids": array of envelope 'ref' ids. Every FACT, INFERENCE and
    RECOMMENDATION entry MUST cite the envelope 'ref' id(s) that license it.
    Entries typed SALUTATION, SIGNATURE_SLOT, NON_SUBSTANTIVE, TRANSITION,
    DISCLOSURE, QUESTION and CTA take "licensed_source_ids": [] when no evidentiary

```

RESPONSE_COMMITMENT facts remain in the semantic envelope, unchanged — this is an

## 3. Exact validator-rule diff

Full unified diff: `docs/readiness/communication-layer/m6.8-3-attempt6-validator-v2.diff`.
Summary of each authorized correction (all gated on `self._v2`):

### 3a — negation-aware demo / availability / response concept detection
- New `_negation_governs(clause, start)`: True when the concept trigger sits
  inside an explicit negation (`not|no|nothing|never|isn't|aren't|doesn't|
  without|nor|non-`) in the 55 chars before it, unless an intervening positive
  deployment verb (`is/are/has deployed/connected/live/…`) cancels it.
- Rule 8 (prohibited-concept scan) and rule 11 (`must_not_say` subset check):
  for `AVAILABILITY_TO_RESPONSE`, `DEMO_DEPLOYED`, `RESPONSE_PERFORMANCE_ASSERTED`
  only, skip a match that is negation-governed. **Unsupported POSITIVE claims
  still fail** — regression `test_positive_demo_deployment_claim_still_fails_v2`
  ("The simulation is now deployed and connected to your intake systems for you.")
  still raises `demo_misrepresented`.
- Rule 11 is also rewritten to be clause-scoped under v2 (the v1 check was
  body-wide subset matching).

### 3b — envelope-authorized-text exemption
- New `_AuthorizedVocab(env)`: the content-word sets of THIS envelope's own
  `required_disclosures.canonical_text`, `structured_cta.canonical_discovery_
  questions`, `mentionable_demo_facts.may_say`, and `structured_cta.semantic_
  frame.asks_for`. Nothing else — not a generic "prompt text is safe" bypass.
- `_matches_authorized(clause, authorized, allow_fold)`: True when the clause is
  ≥70% content-equivalent to one authorized phrase; and (only for
  `AVAILABILITY_TO_RESPONSE` / `RESPONSE_PERFORMANCE_ASSERTED`, where a fold of
  both discovery questions in one CTA sentence is legitimate) when it carries ≥5
  words from the authorized union and introduces ≤2 words beyond it plus the safe
  framing vocabulary. **Not applied to `DEMO_DEPLOYED`** — a genuine positive
  deployment claim shares its content words with the negated disclosure.
- Consulted in rule 8 (the 3 concepts) and rule 17 (`unknown_asserted`).

### 3c — strength reconciliation
- Rule 15e: under v2, when `entry.asserted_strength` equals the strongest cited
  source's licensed strength, the entry is **trusted** — the lexical
  `_detected_strength` guess is not allowed to override a declaration that
  already matches its source. A genuine escalation (declaration ABOVE the source)
  is still caught by 15d (`claim_manifest_strength_mismatch`) and by the
  prohibited-concept scan. Regression: `test_source_aligned_strength_declaration_
  is_trusted_v2` (passes) + `test_genuine_strength_escalation_still_fails_v2`
  (fails).
- `_detected_strength(text, v2=True)` also becomes attribution-aware: "your site
  states X" / "a published note that X" is `PUBLISHED_SELF_CLAIM`, not
  `VERIFIED_FACT`, even with a response verb.

### 3d — business identity vocabulary
- `_licensed_framing_vocab(env, v2=True)` adds THIS envelope's own
  `business_identity.exact_public_hostname` (both the hyphenated token
  `bayline-air-demo` and the split components `bayline`, `air`, `demo`), `www`,
  and the `name_tokens`. No arbitrary external hostname. Regression:
  `test_own_hostname_in_vocab_but_unrelated_hostname_is_not_v2`.

### 3e — disclosure negation grammar
- Rule 6: `_V2_DISCLOSURE_NOT_DEPLOYED` accepts `not | isn't | aren't | wasn't |
  weren't | doesn't | don't | never | no` before `deployed / connected / official
  / operated / a system / implemented / live / integrated / in production`, while
  still requiring the word "simulation" and a disclosure cue. Regression:
  `test_disclosure_negation_grammar_isnt_passes` (passes) +
