## 2. Telemetry-policy determination

Re-read `docs/adr/0079-m6.5-one-link-first-contact-exception.md` line by
line. Its enumerated "Permitted only under all of the following" list
prohibits, specifically: tracking *parameters* in the URL, a click-tracking
*redirect*, an analytics *pixel* **in the message**, and a personalized
*secret/token* **in the URL**. It does not state "the linked demo's hosting
platform must carry zero analytics of any kind." The "Risk" section frames
the concern as "a tracking parameter or redirect slipping into the URL" —
i.e., something this pipeline could add or fail to strip, not a third-party
PaaS's own baseline session-recording template.

Checked against the actual bound URL: no query string, no redirect (direct
HTTP 200), no pixel added to the *message*. **Determination: the literal
text of ADR-0079 does not require zero host-platform telemetry, and is not
a blocker here.** `HOST_PLATFORM_TELEMETRY_CONFIRMED_PRESENT` is preserved
unchanged and recorded explicitly (not silently dropped or upgraded to
"no tracking") in every package artifact below. Proofline added no tracking
of its own.
